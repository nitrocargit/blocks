Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

This policy explains what blox.party (“we”) collects when you play, on blox.party or inside a game portal such as CrazyGames, and why. You don't need an account to play. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • A visitor cookie: a random ID that keeps your place in the queue and in your party. It lasts a year.
  • An analytics ID: a random ID our analytics tool keeps in your browser, so visits can be counted.
  • Your player name: the name you pick, or your CrazyGames username when you play there logged in. Other players see it.
  • Gameplay: the matches you join and what happens in them (moves, kills, beds, results). We record match replays to fix bugs and look into cheating.
  • Chat: messages you send go to the other players in your match and are part of the match replay. A word filter hides profanity.
  • Connection data: your IP address and its approximate location (country, region), and how fast you reach each of our server regions, so we can put you in a nearby match.
  • Analytics: which screens you use, how long loading takes, your browser and device type, and recordings of the menus (not of the game itself). We use PostHog, hosted in the EU, for this.

Your browser also stores your chosen name and settings locally, on your device.

Why

To run the game (matchmaking, parties, keeping matches fair), to fix problems, and to understand how the game is played so we can make it better.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your name, your chat and what you do in a match.
  • Our service providers process it for us: Cloudflare (hosting and servers) and PostHog (analytics).
  • When you play on CrazyGames, CrazyGames handles your CrazyGames account and the data its platform collects under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Analytics and match replays are kept for as long as they are useful for the purposes above, and no longer than two years. You can clear the visitor cookie and stored settings at any time from your browser.

Children

The game is for a general audience. We don't ask for real names, email addresses or other contact details, and we don't knowingly collect more than described here.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@thinking.games. Include your player name and roughly when you played so we can find your data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
